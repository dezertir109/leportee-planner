    How this works. Effects are multivariate estimates from Booking.com listings scraped in October 2026 — each facility's association with the review score holding price, star class, size, review volume, postcode region, local market density and the number of facilities listed constant, with errors clustered by city. They are observational, not guarantees; only effects that survive false-discovery correction and a city-cluster bootstrap are used. Costs are indicative German mid-market figures. The full method is in Le Portée.
